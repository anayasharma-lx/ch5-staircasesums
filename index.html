Staircase Sums

Take a few consecutive numbers like 3 + 4 + 5 + 6 and stack them as a staircase of dots. The staircase has a hidden secret: you can always rearrange it into a rectangle, and that rectangle's area is the sum. Slide to change the start and length — watch the staircase, the rectangle, and the running total all move together.

Why powers of 2 fail: a staircase of length L starting at a sums to L·a + L(L−1)/2. If you double the sum: 2N = L·(2a + L − 1). One of L and (2a + L − 1) is always odd, but powers of 2 have no odd factor bigger than 1 — so no valid L exists.
